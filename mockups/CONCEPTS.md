# PrintCast first-visit concepts

Three static mockups. Same mock bench, three visual languages. View only. No printer controls. Not for deploy.

Shared data: P2S printing `bench-bracket.3mf` at 42% (layer 86 / 240), nozzle 238 / 240 °C, bed 80 / 80 °C, chamber 36 °C. AMS-A: PETG `#4C8DFF` 72%, PLA `#F2F2F2` 40%, ASA empty, Support `#D8B15A` 90%. Six finished files on the tray.

## 1 · Atelier

**Concept.** A control-room that you cannot touch. First paint is a living technical drawing of the P2S with the AMS on top. Telemetry is mapped onto the object: chamber light, heated nozzle tip, warm bed, a PETG volume growing on the plate, four illustrated spools in their real colors. Type sits beside the machine like an engraved plate. Finished work is a steel tray under the fold.

**Wow.** You meet the printer as a thing, not a dashboard of cards.

**Illustrations.** Orthographic chassis, glass chamber, AMS wells, colored reels, heat halo, progress ghost.

**Motion.** Chamber light breathes. Active spools turn slowly. The print volume rises to 42% on load. Heat halo on nozzle and bed. All of it stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 2 · Edition

**Concept.** A paper-walled studio. First paint is a proof sheet of everything made on this bench. The live job is one tile, larger only because it is still becoming. Temps and AMS colors sit in a quiet footer, not a HUD. Serif titles, warm stock, no machine chrome.

**Wow.** You walk into a body of work. The printer is a guest on the wall.

**Illustrations.** Geometric editions of each file on a plate; the live tile holds a miniature chamber plus four true-color wells.

**Motion.** Tiles develop from an underexposed proof (about 1.1s, staggered). No looping chrome.

## 3 · Reel

**Concept.** A cinema gate. Prints are frames on a horizontal film with sprocket holes. The live printer is the frame in the gate, tagged with layer timecode. Scrub the reel to walk the portfolio. Instruments and AMS reels sit under the film as a caption strip.

**Wow.** Everything he has made is one film, and the printer is still rolling.

**Illustrations.** Sprocket film, live-frame machine thumbnail, four caption reels in real filament colors.

**Motion.** The film drifts. The live frame breathes like a projector gate. Native horizontal snap on mobile. Motion off when reduced.

## Pitches

**Atelier.** If the site should feel like standing in front of the machine, pick this: the P2S illustration is the UI, AMS colors are physical reels, and the portfolio is what already left the bed.

**Edition.** If the site should feel like a maker’s show, pick this: the first second is a wall of prints, and the live job is simply the proof that is not dry yet.

**Reel.** If the site should feel like time, pick this: you scrub a film of every object from this bench, and the current print is the frame still in the gate.
